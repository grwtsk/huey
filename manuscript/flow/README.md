# Huey pre-partition flow

This directory is the public working surface for Huey material that belongs to the developing world of the book but does not yet have a final chapter, title, order or boundary.

Governing architecture: [#404](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/404).
Centerpiece composition: [#398](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/398).
Later partition: [#402](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/402).

## Contract

- A file here is a **working flow draft**, not a final chapter.
- A filename or heading is a locator/working descriptor, not final literary identity.
- New material may cross several current chapter candidates.
- Experience precedes argument; later argument cuts may reference the same experience without duplicating or rewriting its provenance.
- Moving material into or out of this directory requires visible lineage rather than silent replacement.
- Nothing here is emitted into the working book merely because it exists.
- Public-copy permission, evidence status and source provenance remain separate from literary placement.
- Raw restricted evidence does not belong here.
- PR-01 and the protected Edna close remain in force.

The intended composition sequence is:

`world → flow → argument cuts → partition → reading order`

The current scale horizon is approximately 120,000 words, nonbinding. Add what the book needs; do not pad toward a count or cut necessary material merely to remain below one.
