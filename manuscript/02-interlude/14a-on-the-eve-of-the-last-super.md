In September of 2026, the problem ceased to be hypothetical.

OpenAI reported that an internal system had produced an analytical solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem together with a Lean formalization. Its public account says that the effort used groups of coordinating agents with cached-web access and code execution; that the successful Navier–Stokes effort involved on the order of ten thousand concurrent agents; that an Euler result produced earlier in the effort was explicitly supplied to the Navier–Stokes agents; that a further-trained version of the internal model replaced the earlier version during the run; and that Codex was used to consolidate intermediate insights across groups. OpenAI reports 2.7 million agent messages and roughly 130 billion output tokens for the Navier–Stokes effort alone. [OpenAI, “On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem”](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/).
