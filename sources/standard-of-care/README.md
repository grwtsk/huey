# Standard-of-care working sources

> [!WARNING]
> **WORKING SOURCE CORPUS — VERIFICATION INCOMPLETE.** These files preserve attributed testimony, allegations, hypotheses, normative analysis and AI-assisted drafts. Inclusion does not establish the truth of a claim or make received medical assertions safe to follow. This is not a clinical protocol, medical/legal advice, adjudication or a current risk assessment. Claims needing evidence are tracked under [#125](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/125), [#145](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/145) and [#176](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/176).

Public copies were expressly approved in [SOC-PUBLIC-01](../../planning/standard-of-care/AUTHORIZATION.md). Raw clinical records and unrelated private material are not included. Original neurology files and the live Reader gate are unchanged.

## Read the corpus

[Conversation context](context/README.md) preserves the progression from pain-limited function, through medical hypotheses and corrections, to standards, burden and trust. It distinguishes exact user statements, assistant-prepared synthesis and proposed literary drafts; it is not an automated full transcript.

[Essays and factual prologue](neurology-text/README.md) contains **twelve complete authored-text exports** from the pinned source version: four prologue entries and eight essays. Each export identifies its containing source blob and preserves original text below a separate repository notice.

[Faith and Medical Ethics Atlas](neurology-current/README.md) preserves **the complete structured model, all 23 principles and their existing identifiers and relations**, normalized to compact JSON. Cross-faith correspondence is not proof of disputed events or doctrinal equivalence.

[Ancillary source review, SOC-T02](../../planning/standard-of-care/ancillary-r02/README.md) adds **five complete, byte-identical source JSON records**: the audio/composition record, two quotation-bearing records, the painting record, and source-site collection metadata. The earlier inventory's classification of *Discipline And Punish* as a quotation is corrected in the review, not by rewriting the source. *On Dignity* remains a template-only preview. The painting image and remote recording are not included by this pass.

[Authority review, SOC-Q03](../../planning/standard-of-care/authority-q03/README.md) adds the **complete byte-identical twelve-entry authority register** and separately records fresh external source checks: nine wording matches in retrieved primary-page text, two official search-excerpt-only results, and one specified edition not verified. The original register and its historical verification labels remain unchanged. [The standards are not silent](../../planning/standard-of-care/authority-q03/argument-draft.md) is a source-based candidate continuation, not an applied manuscript edit.

[First-transfer manifest](transfer-manifest.json) is the historical first-pass receipt. [SOC-T02](transfer-r02.json) supersedes its five completed ancillary rows; [SOC-Q03](transfer-q03.json) supersedes the authority-register row. **Current ancillary remainder: ethics architecture and painting image.** The twelve prose exports' full presentation JSON, automated text comparisons, complete conversation capture and full-corpus semantic review also remain. These are work items, not unresolved permission questions. The copied `site.json` is documentary content, not active Huey configuration or new branding.

## Claim support

[188 named initial targets](../../planning/standard-of-care/claims.tsv) link to their verification issues; **111 substantial-support targets** are coordinated through [#176](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/176). The separate [ANC-C001–070 ledger](../../planning/standard-of-care/ancillary-r02/claims.tsv) adds **70 scoped checks, including 16 substantial-support items**, without renumbering the original SOC targets. The [node index](../../planning/standard-of-care/ancillary-r02/nodes.tsv) preserves all 59 ID-bearing nodes in the five-file selection. Exact spans, repeated occurrences, non-claim labels and review limits remain distinct.

[Q03 field coverage](../../planning/standard-of-care/authority-q03/fields.tsv) accounts for all **271 scalar fields**, including **68 conceptual relationship references** and **89 substantial field-review targets**. These are not claimed as 271 atomic facts. [Fourteen separately stated propositions](../../planning/standard-of-care/authority-q03/claims.tsv) support the twelve-unit candidate. [#192](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/192) retains unresolved text/version/context work; #144 retains correspondence review. Prior and new count categories must not be summed as unique verified claims.

[#145](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/145) tracks remaining atomic-claim and source-unit review. New [#189](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/189) and [#190](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/190) address media/quotation provenance and source-status/citation questions. [#191](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/191) carries the source-based argument about making as communication. A whole paragraph is not automatically one atomic claim, and a source's verification label is not inherited as independent verification.

The source text is not silently rewritten when a claim is corrected. Preserve source, interpretation, correction and contrary evidence separately. Historical crisis language belongs to its source date and is not a declaration about the author's current condition. A personal trauma comparison is not an allegation of a different act that the source expressly disclaims.
