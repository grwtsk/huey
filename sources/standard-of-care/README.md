# Standard-of-care working sources

> [!WARNING]
> **WORKING SOURCE CORPUS — VERIFICATION INCOMPLETE.** These files preserve attributed testimony, allegations, hypotheses, normative analysis and AI-assisted drafts. Inclusion does not establish the truth of a claim or make received medical assertions safe to follow. This is not a clinical protocol, medical/legal advice, adjudication or a current risk assessment. Claims needing evidence are tracked under [#125](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/125), [#145](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/145) and [#176](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/176).

Public copies were expressly approved in [SOC-PUBLIC-01](../../planning/standard-of-care/AUTHORIZATION.md). Raw clinical records and unrelated private material are not included. Original neurology files and the live Reader gate are unchanged.

## Read the corpus

[Conversation context](context/README.md) preserves the progression from pain-limited function, through medical hypotheses and corrections, to standards, burden and trust. It distinguishes exact user statements, assistant-prepared synthesis and proposed literary drafts; it is not an automated full transcript.

[Essays and factual prologue](neurology-text/README.md) contains **twelve complete authored-text exports** from the pinned source version: four prologue entries and eight essays. Each export identifies its containing source blob and preserves original text below a separate repository notice.

[Faith and Medical Ethics Atlas](neurology-current/README.md) preserves **the complete structured model, all 23 principles and their existing identifiers and relations**, normalized to compact JSON. Cross-faith correspondence is not proof of disputed events or doctrinal equivalence.

[Transfer manifest](transfer-manifest.json) identifies source version, representations copied and pending ancillary data. The prose exports are not copies of the full presentation JSON. The rendering-unit maps, architecture, small quote/artwork records, image and shared metadata remain a distinct pending transfer/audit task, not an unresolved permission question.

## Claim support

[188 named initial targets](../../planning/standard-of-care/claims.tsv) link to their verification issues; **111 substantial-support targets** are coordinated through [#176](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/176). [#145](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/145) tracks remaining atomic-claim and source-unit review. Every new material proposition must be linked to an appropriate verification issue; a whole paragraph is not automatically one atomic claim.

The source text is not silently rewritten when a claim is corrected. Preserve source, interpretation, correction and contrary evidence separately. Historical crisis language belongs to its source date and is not a declaration about the author's current condition. A personal trauma comparison is not an allegation of a different act that the source expressly disclaims.
