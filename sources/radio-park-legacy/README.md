## Status

These files are **author-supplied legacy source transcriptions**, not current canonical Huey manuscript and not independent verification of every proposition they contain.

The author supplied the underlying PDFs in conversation on 2026-09-24 and explicitly directed that they be added to the Huey repository, with issues opened for later incorporation.

The connected GitHub write surface accepts text objects but not direct binary attachment upload. Accordingly this directory preserves:

- text-faithful PDF transcriptions;
- explicit PDF page markers;
- original supplied filenames;
- exact SHA-256 hashes of the source PDFs;
- source-specific incorporation issues;
- a visible boundary between legacy source and later Huey adaptation.

The original PDF attachments remain the visual/layout source of record for this intake. Generated/illustrative portraits, typography, page layout and other visual-only material are not reproduced in these Markdown transcriptions.

## Privacy and portrayal

Several legacy pages state that the Radio Park stories represent real people while sensitive details were adjusted to protect privacy. Treat that statement as operative source context.

Therefore:

- do not reverse-identify a person from a legacy literary name;
- do not assume a current remembered name and an older published name are the same person without source support;
- do not present legacy interior monologue as independently recorded testimony;
- do not convert a privacy-protective adaptation into a factual biography by repetition;
- preserve the distinction among observed event, remembered speech, literary reconstruction, source claim, hypothesis and later analysis.

The author's current statement that one supplied story is based on him does not by itself identify which fictionalized or protected narrator is autobiographical. [#415](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/415) owns that provenance question.

## Incorporation law

The corpus follows Huey's current sequence:

`world → source → flow → argument cuts → partition → reading order`

An older story title does not establish a final Huey chapter. A legacy narrator does not automatically become a present narrator. A source proposition does not become verified fact because it is vivid, repeated or useful.

Current adaptation should favor:

- concrete scene before thesis;
- observable gesture before inferred interior state;
- allusive scripture over proof-text where the scene can carry the theology;
- exact source status for mathematical, medical, legal, historical, civic and theological claims;
- moral simultaneity without reducing a whole person to a single act or category;
- preservation of names, uncertainty and privacy boundaries rather than retrospective certainty.

## Verification boundary

Working source corpus; verification and literary incorporation remain incomplete. Nothing in this directory by itself establishes:

- factual truth of every narrated event;
- a diagnosis or clinical conclusion;
- legal or institutional adjudication;
- a mathematical theorem;
- a theological conclusion binding the reader;
- current policy endorsement;
- manuscript acceptance;
- promotion to `main`;
- finished-edition release.
