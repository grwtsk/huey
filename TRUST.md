## The governing question

When a person entrusts a body, a statement, a confidence, a task, a gift or a future possibility to another person or system, **what exactly has been entrusted; who can do what with it; what must remain intact; who carries the work; and how can the person discover, interrupt, correct or leave what happens next?**

Trust is not an instruction to believe every proposition. It is not submission to an institution, an author, an expert, a model or a beautiful argument. It is not one universal legal relationship, a score for a person, or a currency that makes unlike obligations interchangeable.

Trust can be offered through ordinary participation long before defensive record-building begins. Its loss does not erase the earlier offer. A request for safeguards is not proof that a person never trusted. Nor must a person resume emotional confidence before others perform responsibilities they already possess.

The letter begins with three inexpensive-looking acts and ends with something the author can carry. Huey must preserve that descent: from professed value to an identifiable act that changes what a person can safely do. The scale may expand; the human consequence must remain locatable.

## Fourteen connected meanings, not fourteen new bureaucracies

### TR-01 — Agency: trust does not transfer the person

Keep the author's authority over intended testimony and consent distinct from a clinician's assessment, another witness's observations, an investigator's work and a court's findings. Preserve disagreement under its own authorship. Neither silence nor an unanswered complaint transfers exclusive truth to the other participant.

Presence, authentication, dependence, a gift, an earlier yes, a typed submission and a closed issue are not interchangeable permissions. Assistance should enlarge agency rather than acquire it. A support person is a person and participant, not an unappointed replacement decision-maker or the institution's unpaid communication department.

Apply through #2–#14, #40, #140, #165 and the existing operation/authority work under #347. Do not reopen settled author choices or manufacture a new question for routine work already authorized.

### TR-02 — Safety: establish the means of stopping before it is needed

The letter supplies three patient-specific requests: identify expected participants and roles before examination; orient the patient to an accessible exit, assistance and a safe pause; and establish a usable written or nonverbal panic/pause/stop channel before distress compromises expression. Preserve all three. An offered appointment, an explanation before the first touch, or permission to bring a device does not by itself demonstrate their implementation.

A proposed note is not an entered note. An entered note is not proof that staff could find or follow it. Identify which step is proposed, performed, verified or unresolved. Limited speech, delayed response or little outward change must not silently become affirmative consent in Huey's representations of this request.

The final communication aid is minimum-necessary, durable and usable without portal navigation during distress, supported by clinician review rather than an agent's signature. It is not a DNR, an instruction to withhold emergency stabilization, or a reason to explain away new symptoms. Existing owners include #250–#252, #257 and #266–#268.

### TR-03 — Reception: communication is an exchange, not an upload

Trace question → reception → processing → qualification → expression → faithful receipt → attributable response. The sequence is a design aid, not a claim that every real exchange follows a rigid linear machine. Extra articulation time alone may not address inaccessible spoken input. Written questions, paced exchange, delayed qualification and usable alternatives should be evaluated at the task level.

Letters, books, art, technical research and ordinary discussion belong to the author's established communication history. They are not decorative evidence of exceptional intelligence or an obligation to endorse every proposition they contain. Preserve their actual purpose and limits. A clinician need not read unlimited material for a reasonable pre-visit submission to reach its intended recipient.

Apply through #42/#43/#67/#123/#138/#191 and #244–#255. Digital conformance, actual usability, communication effectiveness and continuity are related but different findings. A portal's existence or login success settles none of the others.

### TR-04 — Provenance: a representation must remain answerable to its source

Distinguish testimony, separately attributed witness evidence, contemporaneous records, inherited assertions, later recollection, measurements, hypotheses, analysis and adjudicated findings. Preserve the actual date and version of each. Repetition does not create independence; polished presentation does not create support.

The original source remains unchanged. Corrections belong to an identified derivative or amendment with before/after lineage. A later correction must also reach the current places where the superseded proposition would otherwise remain active. An unavailable source is not an absent event; a source mentioned in a letter is not thereby independently inspected.

Use the existing #18, SOC, ANC and HUEY-CARELAW namespaces and their verification owners. This standard does not create a competing claim registry or turn a new checklist into evidence.

### TR-05 — Confidence and stewardship: custody is not possession

Keep confidentiality, access, purpose, retention and redistribution separately accountable. A patient, witness, artist or researcher may entrust something for one use without permitting every other use. Public Git includes branches, discussions, logs and artifacts; an ignored folder is not a confidential vault.

Keep raw restricted evidence, private locators and unauthorized fingerprints on their permitted surfaces. Use existing grants rather than requesting consent repeatedly. Ask only about a genuinely new consequential action. Availability, a checksum and an account name do not supply missing permission, authenticity or authorship.

The evidence-vault boundary and #2 remain controlling. This integration activates no replica, new storage destination, source service or outside disclosure.

### TR-06 — Professional undertaking: a role has a scope and a next action

For each consequential responsibility, identify the actor, actual role, relevant knowledge, applicable undertaking or rule, available action, limits, competent reviewer and next responsible path. A professional boundary may delimit one person's task without causing the patient's need to disappear.

Clinical, ethical, institutional, contractual, statutory, regulatory and judicial standards cannot do one another's work. A mission statement is not performance; a billing code is not the entire standard-of-care inquiry; a title is not proof of diligence. Record a reasoned refusal and an effective alternative where appropriate rather than substituting the word standard for analysis.

Preserve both individual conduct and possible institutional constraints as examinable. Support for clinicians does not acquit a particular omission; criticism of an omission does not exhaust a clinician's personhood. #26/#139/#170/#183/#184/#327/#392/#452 retain their scopes.

### TR-07 — Reciprocity: follow the burden, not only the invoice

Distinguish institutional spending, physician salary-equivalent time, overhead, permissible charges, patient/family labor and nonmonetary loss. Unknown work is not zero. A task is not efficient merely because its cost is moved outside the institution's ledger. Do not make the author produce an exhaustive unpaid time study before recognizing an already-described burden.

Preserve the September 30 $8.76 illustration separately from the historical $10.46 benchmark. Neither is Dr. Bledsoe's verified salary, an actual invoice, a damages award or a price for dignity. The reported AGI comparison is an explicitly mixed-basis illustration of relative burden, not a rule requiring another person to pay a matching amount. A proposed good-faith tender is not known payment or assent to a disability surcharge.

Computation remains #30; legal fee questions remain #184; institutional allocation remains #327; narrative use remains #453. Do not create a second economic model or silently replace the older scenario.

### TR-08 — Notice and handoff: name the state actually reached

Reuse #324/#325. Authored, sent, delivered, available, accessible, reviewed, understood, acknowledged, agreed and acted upon are distinct states. Do not infer the later state from the earlier one. The institutional recipient deserves accurate notice-state attribution too.

A handoff needs a responsible recipient, necessary information, an accessible route, an acceptance or other attributable disposition, and a next step. An order placed or a directory supplied is not automatically a completed handoff. Preserve an unanswered question as unanswered, not as proof that no internal work occurred.

Requested response periods in the letter are not universal statutory clocks. No deadline begins in this repository because the letter was drafted here. Preparation for outside routing is not permission to transmit medical records or contact officials.

### TR-09 — Continuity and repair: make scrutiny capable of ending

Trustworthiness is shown through completed, inspectable work: a communication plan usable at the next encounter, a correction visible where needed, a referral accepted or responsibly redirected, an accessible summary received, a defect repaired without loss of authorship.

A useful repair need not compel forgiveness, restore the untouched past or erase accountability. Good care and an unresolved complaint can coexist. Present safety need not wait for historical adjudication; historical review must not be declared satisfied because present care improves.

The purpose is not endless adversarial maintenance by the patient. Prefer a finite, durable result that lets the person return to ordinary life. #71/#174/#300/#386 and the care return in #448 retain that purpose.

### TR-10 — Reader trust: offer an intelligible world, not compulsory assent

The reader must be able to distinguish whose words and conclusions are encountered without being buried in repetitive disclaimers. Put material qualifications where their absence would mislead. Let scenes and actions carry meaning; give the reader space to recognize, question or disagree.

Neither sympathy nor a reader's similar experience independently proves this history. Do not make every possible answer confirm the charge. Preserve care, humor, beauty, ordinary ambition and contradiction as parts of life, not just instruments of prosecution or vindication.

RF-01 permits severe actual testimony; it does not authorize an agent-invented apology or self-rebuke. PR-01, the protected final excursion and its terminal placement remain unchanged. No explanatory trust coda, medical card or litigation return follows that close.

### TR-11 — Relation, faith and gift: receive without owning

Books lent, art given, family care, faith, naming, finance recollections and philanthropic stewardship can illuminate different forms of entrusting. Do not collapse them into an exchange that purchases agreement, clinical endorsement, personal loyalty or silence.

Within Huey's existing theological work, relation need not erase difference, embodiment need not erase consent, naming need not overwrite history, communion need not create possession, and hope need not promise a cure. These are source-bound theological/editorial relations, not medical mechanisms or universal legal conclusions.

A wife, child, teacher, community or Black intellectual tradition has purposes beyond this narrator's case. H447-D01 remains operative: the narrator's work serves the independent visibility and agency of Black thought, rather than making it evidence owed to him. #28/#330/#331/#388–#400/#458/#459 retain their specific source and review obligations.

### TR-12 — Public trust and law: enlarge accountability without merging actors

Carry the letter's movement from one patient's usable safeguards toward examination of shared barriers. The public question is whether responsible systems can make rights practically exercisable, not whether one patient earns an exceptional private favor.

Preserve the letter's request for counsel to evaluate class-wide remedies as a request, not an existing certified class, a filed action or a finding of prevalence. California v. California is its rhetorical accountability frame, not a case caption. Counties, public offices, UC, UCSF entities and individual clinicians do not become one defendant because they appear in the same argument.

Specific legal trusts, fiduciary duties, contracts, public stewardship and personal reliance have distinct predicates and forums. Preserve jurisdiction, time, elements, exceptions and competent review under #184/#325/#327/#452. No political verdict, coercive fundraising, money-for-silence bargain or outside filing is created here.

### TR-13 — Technical and mathematical trust: bind only what the mechanism binds

Separate integrity, identity, authentication, authorization, availability, semantic fidelity, source support, formal proof and human acceptance. A hash binds bytes. A test checks a declared property. A theorem establishes its stated result under its actual assumptions. None independently certifies a remembered encounter or grants publication permission.

In the editor, preserve proposed/local/submitted/acknowledged/conflicted/cancelled states, stable identity, exact source versions and accountable effects. Do not promote a locally queued operation into an accepted remote change or let a rendering change mutate source meaning. Preserve important qualifications in text, listening, export, fallback and assistive paths.

Keep the complete editorial inventory distinct from the publication projection. Unknown, pending, restricted and unmaterialized do not mean nonexistent. Do not introduce another canonical prose store. #347–#370 and #455 own implementation; this standard does not claim those implementations complete.

### TR-14 — Independence: scrutiny must be able to change a conclusion

Preserve favorable, contrary, limiting and contextual evidence as different relations to an exact proposition. Let correction matter. A reviewer needs an identifiable remit and appropriate source access; the subject of a complaint should not silently become the sole interpreter of whether independent review occurred.

Respect first-person testimony without converting conviction into external proof. Respect expertise without giving an institutional record automatic priority over all other sources. Do not assign credibility by fluency, composure, income, disability, race, professional success or a numerical trust score.

Unknown motive or classification does not erase a supported relationship. Equally, affiliation, a donor's name or a repeated allegation does not establish a transmission path. This rule protects the inquiry in both directions.

## Use in an issue or pull request

Choose the meanings actually implicated by the work. In a short existing acceptance note, identify the entrusted thing, the actor/channel, the action or boundary, the person carrying its cost, the source/status distinction that must survive, and the observable result. State a material counterexample or remaining unknown where relevant. Reuse the existing human–system tuple:

`<human, system, channel, authority, burden, record, evidence, remedy>`

Do not require every issue to recite all fourteen meanings. Do not add a permission ceremony or force the author to retell trauma. Resolve available facts and ordinary coordination within the authorized work. A trust addendum is an acceptance requirement for the affected task, not a declaration that the task is done.
