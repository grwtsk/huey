# Certificates

This directory is a public **certificate repository**, not an evidence repository.

Certificates are derived, reviewable records of bounded checks. They may refer to approved opaque source IDs, claim IDs, manuscript paths, public commit IDs, build inputs, verifier versions, dates, results, and limitations.

Do not place raw evidence here. Do not commit clinical records, PDFs, screenshots, photographs, audio, private correspondence, restricted quotations, private locators, credentials, access tokens, or private-source fingerprints that are not separately authorized for public disclosure.

Raw evidence remains in the separately controlled private evidence repository/service. A certificate may say that a defined check was performed against a private source version without reproducing or exposing that source.

Certificates are Markdown-only at this stage. Use template.md and keep source, claim, and build certificates in their corresponding subdirectories.

A certificate does not establish the truth of an underlying event, legal liability, medical validity, or publication clearance. It records only the check it actually describes.
