## Capturing new relationships

Use the existing evidence ID and claim/incident/issue IDs. For each proposed
relationship record `supports`, `contradicts`, `limits`, `context`, `duplicate`,
`same_source_family`, `supersedes`, `provenance_only` or `unresolved`, an exact
permitted locator, what it contributes and what it does not establish. Private
locators and detailed assessment remain private unless separately permitted.
Existence-sensitive items may have no public certificate at all.

A newly deposited document starts review; it does not automatically endorse or
invalidate the author's or institution's account. Preserve independent authorship,
source family, contrary evidence and correction lineage. Receipt, read,
assessment and resolution are distinct. No empty public map, missing record,
unknown operator or unresolved classification means zero, omission or absence.

The [vault handoff](vault-handoff.md) carries capture receipts. Later #314 work
must bind an assessed relationship to the exact manuscript version/range. Local
reader edits and changed paragraph source versions cannot inherit old support
without reconciliation. Main remains held pending the separate exact-version
journalistic review and actual human decision.
