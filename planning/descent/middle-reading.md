## Attention arrives after need

The birth-room passage supplies a change that the reader can locate: the narrator
enters, the doctors notice him, and their attention turns toward the mother. Her
need precedes his arrival. His whiteness and the direction of attention belong to
the account; replacing them with a generic story about advocacy would remove what
the passage observes. Equally, the scene does not furnish each doctor's thoughts.
Its original language remains untouched. The Atlas's dignity principle supplies
an ethical comparison, not corroboration of the room.

Before this crossing, recognition can sound like an inward achievement. Here it
has an outward direction and someone waiting at its far end. After it, a later
institutional representation can be examined for whom it makes visible and what
it passes over. The question becomes particular: whose need was already present
before the representation changed? It need not begin by supplying an unobserved
motive or making a classification the price of describing the relation.

## Nearness does not merge agency

At the wreck, the narrator separates his slipping finger from the biker's removal
of the door. The events are close in memory; their agents remain distinct. The
woman making the emergency call supplies another action already under way. The
narrator's effort, the biker's strength and the woman's response are not competing
claims to own the rescue.

This changes how the later descent can accumulate accounts. Several people in a
scene need not become one actor. Several representations of a source need not
become several witnesses. The existing source-family card supplies a separate
documentary comparison for that second distinction; it supplies no new witness
to the wreck. A future mathematical adapter must preserve these identities
rather than infer that geometric agreement makes sources independent.

The ambulance subsequently moves beyond the narrator's observation. The stopping
signals do not complete the man's story. This limit is local to that outcome; it
does not retract the actions the narrator has just described. A descent that
keeps adding interpretive layers must retain that distinction between a known
relationship and an unavailable next event.

## An arrangement has a receiving end

The Fresno passage keeps several things together: a doctor's organized transfer
preparation, her first child's birth, a sincere apology, and the narrator's
continuing lack of access to primary care. None has to erase the others. It is
precisely because preparation and apology remain present that the distance to
care received becomes perceptible.

The Atlas calls this problem fidelity and follow-through. The existing
SOC-C113–SOC-C121 review targets ask for an actual referral, recipient response,
alternatives, responsibility and completion. Those targets provide questions for
comparison; they are not a finding about this recalled transfer, nor proof that
the Stanford-to-UCSF history is the same event. The corresponding essay supplies
the author's separate account of what crossed an institutional boundary.

The admitted correspondence card distinguishes a draft and workflow status from
transmission and downstream action. Used beside the scene, it makes the next
documentary question more exact. What is evidenced at each step? A prepared
document, its receipt, its review and its implementation are different answers.
An unknown step remains unknown; it cannot be completed by literary sequence.

## Receiving a report leaves room for a response

In the telephone passage, the narrator asks for his report of an absence of care
to be received and routed. He distinguishes that request from requiring the
staff member to decide abandonment or law. Her actual authority and training
remain unknown in the narrative. That unknown does not remove the encounter or
the need for a receiving route.

The care-law R25 review row preserves the separate questions of clinical care
and allegation routing. Its historical source-review status is not fresh legal
verification. The practical return for #448 can already be distinguished through
existing targets: SOC-I057 concerns advance participant names and roles; SOC-I056
concerns the agenda and a narrower route for the patient's material; SOC-I073
concerns preparation actually performed. A proposed chart flag would be a fourth,
proposed action until its entry and use were evidenced. Writing the flag here
would not show that anyone entered, read or acted on it.

Present preparation can be examined without resolving every historical dispute.
That does not make preparation an answer to the whole accountability inquiry.
Each has a subject, a possible recipient and a different completion condition.

## The readable sentence conceals work

The hand that touched the door is the hand negotiating the keyboard. C08A brings
the reader back to the stiffness, pauses and minimum movement that disappear
from a fluent reading. The author's essay *Writing as the Act of Care* supplies a
separate account of communication preserving participation during a dated crisis.
Its broader clinical propositions remain with their review owners. The crossing
does not turn a remembered episode into a universal intervention, a present risk
assessment or a measurement of typing time.

The literary consequence is immediate: an additional request for explanation
asks for work from a body. SOC-C134, SOC-C138 and SOC-C142 provide existing routes
for examining tasks, time and effects. The source does not price them. The $10.46
comparison therefore keeps its separate calculation obligation under #30/#453.

## Correction can change the descent

The admitted measurement card retains a printed 6 cm statement and an attributed
reply correcting it to 6 mm. The original report's amendment and an independent
image measurement are not supplied by that reply. The correction nevertheless
exists in the selected public account and changes any assertion that nobody
responded to this particular error. It must not be omitted to produce a closed
sequence in which every representation only perpetuates harm.

This is a documentary counterpressure beside the chapter's rescue, apology and
ongoing care questions. It neither proves that all corrections were completed
nor acquits unrelated conduct. It shows where the next traversal must branch:
what was answered, what changed, and what still requires an answer?

## The mathematical insertion remains specific

C08A contains the fractional stroke and immediately identifies it as remembered
movement. That gives #447 an exact literary occurrence to preserve. It supplies
neither a derivation of the requested 1/(2π) claim nor an identity between the swim,
a mathematical source coordinate and the narrative center. Repetition of the
constant would not supply the missing relation.

The next formal return must name the actual object, coordinate, derivative
convention, order, orientation, normalization, hypotheses and verification state.
The candidate adapter must say which separately attributed relations it encodes,
what its operations preserve and what they omit. Finite data, all-order existence
and all-rank sign claims cannot exchange their statuses. The seven crossings in
this packet are an editorial selection, **not seven derivative entries**.

The resulting reading path is usable before that adapter is complete: approach
the middle with people and separately attributed accounts; leave it able to
distinguish selection from erasure, proximity from shared agency, preparation
from completion, and a correction from a finished remedy. Then return each
expanded question to the person, task and receiving action it concerns. This
does not assign final chapter cuts or add anything after the protected close.
