## What the capstone gathers

This is a narrative culmination, not a proof that substitutes for unresolved evidence. Bring together the narrator's position within an arrangement, the visibility of a small event, faithful retention rather than erasure, another person's irreducibility, and the responsibility that follows recognition. Let earlier work carry those meanings; do not restate every argument or insert a survey of theorists at the threshold.

The narrator is subject to the same provenance discipline applied to other participants. His genuine love and resistance do not require erasing what he remembers. The confession does not compel someone else to reassure, forgive, answer or demonstrate innocence. It does not make all errors and harms equivalent. No other person is conscripted as evidence of his redemption.
