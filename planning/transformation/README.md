## Preserve different records and times

The supplied evidence-entry template is a **private working analysis** template.
Completed copies remain private unless the exact version, action and destination
are separately authorized under #2: its fragment, people, private locator, raw
digest, chronology and quotations can reveal protected information.
The existing vault manifest and public certificate schemas remain unchanged.
The proposal's narrative-occurrence record type is absent from the YAML enum,
and its proposition section has no separate limiting/contextual source lists.
Use the existing nine-type relationship contract below for those distinctions;
do not replace the compatible vault schema with the proposed template.

| Proposed working record | Existing contract / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Source and original | Vault evidence ID, immutable bytes and versioned representations; the public certificate is a separately reviewed export |
| Event | Occurrence as supported or reported; preserve exact/approximate/interval/unknown time and source |
| Experience | Separate reported remembering/reliving record; not another independent recording of an earlier event |
| Proposition | Reuse the existing claim, incident or verification-issue ID in working analysis; preserve assertion kind, attribution, support, contrary material and revision conditions |
| Standard | Exact issuing body, operative version, scope and applicability; a representation is not evidence of case-specific compliance |
| Action / handoff | Distinct request, dispatch, delivery, receipt, review, comprehension, referral acceptance, action and effective result; no inferred intermediate steps |
| Narrative occurrence | Existing literary identity and exact represented version/range; reading position is a projection |

Keep underlying event time, remembering time, expression/document time, each
institutional action time, and narrative position separate. An unknown date is
not zero; a missing record is not proof that an action never occurred. Preserve
historical institutional names before adding a separately supported contemporary
entity match. A governance, standards or potential jurisdictional role does not
establish case involvement. Current care/access and retrospective investigation
remain separate tracks.

The minimum private intake remains the fragment, its available origin, and any
uncertainty the contributor wants preserved. Other fields may stay null. A
collaborator organizes existing material without making completion of a form,
chronology, diagnosis or another retelling a condition of receiving testimony.

## Prepare separate questions, not one global conclusion

Keep the reported encounter, subsequent effects, later institutional decisions,
access across institutional boundaries, and the narrator's interpretations as
separately attributable proposition groups. Support for one group does not
automatically settle another. A later access failure can be examined while motive
remains unknown; a disputed record does not by itself identify its author or prove
fraud. Favorable, contrary and limiting material belong beside the exact assertion
they affect. Supply a revision condition for each consequential assertion.

The proposal's two formal models belong in #29/#399 review before manuscript
application. The first concerns whether a recorded representation preserves enough
distinctions for an adequate response; an available clarifying question can matter.
The second concerns whether locally feasible steps compose into an accessible
route; capacity, cumulative burden and interface compatibility need explicit
assumptions. Mathematical validity of a model and evidence that it represents an
actual sequence are separate review questions. No personal capacity measurement,
legal result, causal chain or shared institutional motive follows from these
models alone. I09 should expose the assumptions after the narrative supplies its
source-grounded examples.
