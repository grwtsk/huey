**S6 — Chrysostom, Homily 20 on Second Corinthians, section 3.** https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/220220.htm. **S7 — Prayer traditionally attributed to Ephrem.** https://www.orthodoxprayer.org/LentenPrayer.html. Displayed English and Greek inspected. Use this identified presentation as liturgical reception, with traditional attribution kept distinct from critically established authorship.
