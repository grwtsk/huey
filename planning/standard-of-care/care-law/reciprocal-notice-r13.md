# HUEY-CL13 — reciprocal notice, case-specific communication matrix

**Huey book work.** Reciprocal Effective Notice [#324](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/324); verification [#325](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/325); communication/notice [#180](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/180); records [#181](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/181); routing [#183](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/183); chronology [#187](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/issues/187); development [PR #122](https://github.com/grwtsk/huey/pull/122).

> Working evidence matrix. It distinguishes document existence, stated dispatch, reported delivery, actual receipt, review, accessibility, acknowledgment and agreement. It does not decide legal sufficiency of notice, agency/imputation, waiver, estoppel, informed consent, retaliation, discharge rights, breach or liability.

## Core rule

Use the strongest state the evidence supports and stop there:

`authored → recorded → routed → delivered/available → received → accessible → reviewed → understood/reasonable opportunity → acknowledged → agreed/authorized`

A stronger state requires its own evidence or a specifically applicable legal rule.

This is not mechanical symmetry. Different senders, designated recipients, channels, statutes and consequences may justify different notice rules. The purpose of the matrix is to identify the difference rather than silently promote one state into another.

The asymmetry is therefore evidentiary before it is legal: **the corpus contains direct patient-authored acknowledgment that N04 was received and reviewed, but it does not presently contain comparable institutional acknowledgment or review evidence for N01–N03, N05 or N06.**

That difference may have many explanations:
- evidence exists elsewhere but is not in this corpus;
- the communications reached a queue but not the individual clinician;
- some were scanned or preserved without substantive review;
- some were reviewed without an attributable acknowledgment;
- some were routed to another function;
- some may not have been delivered; or
- institutional workflow may define receipt/review differently.

The matrix does not choose among those explanations without evidence.

## Consequence-specific notice

A single communication can have different notice questions for different consequences.

For N04:
- **notice of the letter's existence:** established by the patient's express acknowledgment;
- **notice of the sixty-day condition:** the detailed September 17 response quotes and contests it;
- **agreement to the conditions as drafted:** not established;
- **prospective willingness to comply with legitimate requirements:** expressly asserted by the patient;
- **agreement with factual characterizations/accommodation determinations:** expressly disputed;
- **institutional acceptance of a qualified response:** unknown.

For patient-authored submissions:
- **existence of the supplied source object:** established in this corpus;
- **actual delivery:** varies by row and evidence;
- **record entry:** unresolved unless an attributable system record is produced;
- **particular clinician review:** unresolved;
- **knowledge of a particular proposition:** cannot be inferred merely from document length or chart presence;
- **acceptance of a requested action:** unresolved.

## What evidence would advance each row

### Delivery and receipt
- certified-mail acceptance/receipt records;
- hand-delivery log;
- envelope/label/USPS tracking tied to the exact object;
- institutional scanning/import event tied to the exact version.

### Routing
- queue/location;
- document type;
- responsible service;
- routing event;
- named recipient/function where lawfully available.

### Review
- acknowledgment;
- response quoting or addressing the material proposition;
- nonprivileged assignment/status metadata;
- clinician/institution statement of materials reviewed.

A timestamp showing that a record was opened may support access to the object; it does not necessarily establish that every proposition was read or understood.

### Agreement
- signature;
- MyChart agreement;
- other authorized affirmative response;
- or a governing rule that gives a different legal effect to specified conduct.

Silence, chart presence and delivery are not automatically substituted for agreement.

## Legal-policy status

Reciprocal Effective Notice remains a proposed fairness/record-stewardship principle, not a universal California doctrine.

This pass intentionally does **not** generalize:
- malpractice pre-suit notice;
- HIPAA access/amendment deadlines;
- consent doctrine;
- grievance rules;
- portal availability;
- certified-mail presumptions; or
- agency/imputation law

into one universal notice rule. Those remain context-specific research under #184/#325.
