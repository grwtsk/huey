## Editorial work

Chapter 12 restores prior trust to the history before later self-advocacy, separates patient endurance from a qualification for care, and gives the author's account of family assistance its source and limits. The burden argument now asks what work was actually repeated, what caused the repetition, where a feasible intervention could have interrupted it, and whether an apparent saving merely moved work onto the patient or support person. A research model is used as a model, not as a diagnosis or an effectiveness result.

The professional trust discussion addresses external constraints as well as individual action. A specific no-surcharge rule remains distinct from the wider moral argument about unpaid labor. The noneconomic-loss discussion does not turn wage proxies into a valuation of pain or a conclusion that a compensable claim has been established.

Chapter 13 now separates receipt, accepted responsibility and completed work in the proposed design. It specifies source and reliance relationships, qualified referral questions, intake and fallback inquiries, and correction along known downstream links. Author-controlled archive permissions remain separate from the legal duties of a clinical record custodian. The four chamber names remain design concerns, not four offices or a replacement for competent clinical, access, ethical, investigative and legal functions.

The chapter identifies the medical-specialist standard without having the narrator adjudicate it, and distinguishes a specified HIPAA complaint process from an entitlement to make one office investigate everything. Closure has a question, evidence, reasons, unresolved scope and review route. No open task, silence, correspondence or proposed interface is represented as extending a deadline or creating consent.

Both revised chapters and their changed joins were reread after assembly. That editorial review is separate from structural tests and is not an independent second review. Original source statements, current proposals and source-specific uncertainty remain distinguishable under RF-01.
