# Evidence intake for the next stage

**Prepared, not activated.** Issue #307. Registration is not contingent on corroboration. Do not contact providers, witnesses, regulators or other people; query new accounts; file requests; schedule monitoring; or publish new raw material merely because this structure exists.

For each authorized received object, preserve the original and record an evidence identifier, source/speaker, version, actual custody, event date, authoring date, dispatch date and receipt date separately. Unknown fields remain unknown. A letter's heading is not proof of dispatch or receipt; an export date is not necessarily an event date.

Link the evidence to one or more SOC-I entries using **supports, contradicts, limits, context, duplicate, or unresolved**. For each link give an exact page/paragraph/field or time range, the proposition it addresses, what it establishes, what it cannot establish, and what remains to be checked. Separate direct observation, attributed testimony, documentary statement, professional assessment and model interpretation. A source can support one proposition while limiting another.

Retain original wording and later correction separately. Do not replace a patient report with a clinician summary or assume that several copied notes are independent observations. Identify the common source family. Distinct actors, acts or time periods may later justify new entries; alternative legal theories do not automatically increase the event count.

Keep **not requested, requested, received, read, assessed, and resolved** separate. A verified checksum means bytes match; it does not prove authorship, delivery, truth or permission. An attachment does not change an entry's finding state. A completed upload, issue closure or passing test is not a clinical, legal or institutional finding.

Collect favorable and unfavorable material for every participant. Check what information was available to each actor at the time, and distinguish absent material from an established absence of action. Use the source already supplied before asking a narrow necessary question. Do not require repeated traumatic retelling as an intake ritual.

Public issue/index access and evidence custody are separate. New evidence does not inherit public-disclosure permission from the approved essays. Raw clinical PDFs, private source identifiers, credentials, temporary access links and signatures remain on an appropriately authorized surface. Public updates can record a source-neutral disposition without exposing the raw object.

The collection stage should produce an evidence-to-entry map, a source/custody inventory, a receipt chronology, a contrary-material log, explicit unresolved gaps and a record of every status change. It should not manufacture an investigation, professional opinion, charge, consent, waiver or edition acceptance.
