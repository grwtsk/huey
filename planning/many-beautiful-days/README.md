## Purpose

Rahel Haile's letter about Lia supplies a positive account of the life Huey must leave room for: learning, organizing a day, family and friendship, two dogs, singing with other people, religious participation, teaching younger children, cooking and playing together, baking gifts, honesty, and joy. These activities have purposes of their own. They are not evidence that the narrator deserves care, not the compensation for an injury, and not ornaments added to make a difficult book easier to receive.

The practical question for work governed by this standard is: **What part of a person's lived day or self-directed future does this work preserve, and how does that person retain authority over it?** Answer at the scope of the issue. A source-checking task may protect accurate attribution; a reader task may protect an uninterrupted, stoppable reading session; a care passage may identify time and participation lost to a failed handoff. Not every task needs another family scene or a new measurement.

This is saturation by consequence, not by repetition. The complete blessing need not recur throughout the manuscript. Its meaning must change what the work notices, preserves, makes possible and refuses to appropriate.

## The four words in practice

### Many

Continuity matters. One permitted message, one successful examination sample, one exported file or one completed task does not exhaust a life. Follow the relevant action through a usable next step and, where the task requires it, repeat use. Do not convert this into a promise of unlimited availability or a demand that the family maintain a new diary to prove ordinary participation.

In composition, give ordinary life more than one function: it can precede an injury, continue beside it, resist an argument, and remain possible afterward. Preserve the actual chronology of every occurrence. Lia's present university preparation is not material that may be backdated into an earlier childhood or clinical scene.

### Beautiful

Value belongs to the person. In this source it includes a mother hearing her daughter's voice, a cake made for a friend, participation in church, and happiness in each other's company. Beauty does not certify virtue, eliminate pain, prove a cure or require an agreeable mood. Neither a visually attractive interface nor a gentle sentence is adequate when it loses meaning, consent or access.

Keep the possibility of enjoyment in the book without requiring it to redeem wrongdoing. Care and harm can coexist without cancelling each other. A person who cannot sing, organize, work, believe, give or appear joyful has no lesser claim to dignity or accessible participation.

### Days

Work with the scale at which life is lived. Daily lists, school responsibilities, rest, attention, family time and the effort behind a message belong in the field of view. Where a process transfers work, identify who must do it and what it displaces, using the actual source. Do not invent minutes, money, missed events or a child's caregiving role.

A successful procedure should not be the only outcome a reader can recognize. The relevant question may be whether someone can return to a chosen activity, finish a thought, hear a song, rest, or spend time with another person. These are purposes, not a mandatory clinical endpoint list or a numerical score for human worth.

### Ahead

Leave the future unowned and unguaranteed. A parent's hopes are not a university admission, a completed achievement, a prognosis or an obligation imposed on the child. Lia may discover interests and friendships not specified by either parent or by Huey. She is not charged with vindicating the narrator, repairing his injuries or carrying the book forward.

Technical and editorial systems should preserve correction, revision, pause, refusal and genuinely new choices. An earlier yes is not permission for every later disclosure or transformation. A preserved history must not prevent a person from exceeding a former description.
