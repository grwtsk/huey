## The controlling connection

The existence of this letter changes the developmental question. Preservation is no longer the endpoint.

The hinge is:

1. **Response:** did the author's disclosure of suicide risk produce an identifiable clinical response?
2. **Capacity:** did the system equip the treating physician to provide, obtain, coordinate, or escalate that response?

These are linked but distinct. Later prose must not collapse them into automatic personal blame or automatic institutional exoneration.

The record may support individual omission, failed handoff, structural constraint, interacting failures, a response unknown to the author, or another explanation. The purpose of reconstruction is to identify the actual path.

## Forceful statement of humility

> **I am not asking medicine to become something other than medicine. I am asking the institutions through which it operates to maintain the connections necessary to protect life. An administrative boundary may divide responsibility. It must not be allowed to make responsibility disappear.**

This is the pivot sentence.

It narrows the demand rather than enlarging it. Neurology need not become Psychiatry. A regulator need not become a treating clinician. A service need not claim authority it does not possess. The connective obligation under examination is whether a material life-safety disclosure reaches an appropriate recipient, whether the next responsibility is identifiable, whether failed acceptance or completion has an escalation route, and whether an accessible response reaches the patient.
