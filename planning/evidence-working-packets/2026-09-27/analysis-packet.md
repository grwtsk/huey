### Observed omissions

- The two representations are overlapping source content, not independent examinations.

### Questions for review

- What contemporaneous evidence distinguishes what was recorded from what was actually performed?

### Proposed relationships

- **limits → SOC-C009** (examination and questionnaire reference): Identifies specific available and missing documentation. Does not establish: Performance or nonperformance of an unmentioned procedure; clinical adequacy or liability.

## File naming and document dates are different kinds of evidence

- **Observed — HUEY-EV-6f73c3c6-dc2f-4b2c-9c4c-a4ea43d0d73a, private acquisition metadata; sensitive filename withheld:** A separately preserved filename suggests a different year.

### Observed omissions

- The received object does not explain the naming discrepancy or authenticate when the filename was assigned.

### Limits and uncertainties

- A filename is not a substitute for a displayed study date.
- Collection time, report time, print time, filesystem time and intake time remain distinct; none alone establishes delivery or review.
- The sensitive filename and precise filesystem metadata are intentionally absent from this proposal.

### Questions for review

- Which source should anchor an event timeline, and which date belongs only to the derivative or custody history?

### Proposed relationships

- **limits → SOC-C004** (pp1–2; filename discrepancy represented without the filename): Separates the displayed study date from acquisition naming. Does not establish: Why the discrepancy arose or an independently authenticated event date.
